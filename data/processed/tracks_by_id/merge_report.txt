Conventions preserved
----------------------------------------------------------------------
  * LEC terms are native 3-hourly, linearly interpolated to 1-hourly
    upstream. 'lec_original' is True on the original values.
  * CPS parameters are native 3-hourly. 'cps_original' marks computed
    values. cps_class is the raw per-timestep threshold label;
    cps_state is the guarded >=36 h persistent state, or empty.

    wherever no wind record matches a catalogue timestep.

    1e-14 deg against the quadrant offsets, not a great-circle distance.
  * The '*_global_quad' columns hold the NAME of the quadrant carrying
    the extremum, not its value.
  * All timestamps are UTC-naive on both sides. No tz conversion.
